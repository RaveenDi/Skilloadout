# Material requirements

Supply an approved headline, a truthful evidence caption and a final action. A product story also needs the owner's permitted logo and legible screenshot or footage of the demonstrated feature. A lesson needs its actual diagram/worked example and checked explanation. Do not manufacture those materials from an unrelated preset.

The bundled SVG is an original optional emphasis shape. Its `currentColor` is assigned by the consuming composition; the motion/design documents define that role. It is not a preview frame or brand logo.

No fonts, screenshots, voice, music or footage are bundled. Generic type is only a recipe default. Before a repeatable production export, supply licensed font files and record their names, versions and permissions in the derived package. Any new third-party asset needs its applicable terms and inventory entry. Missing required evidence blocks a finished-video claim but does not block inspecting the recipe or generating its plan.

The optional Node helper has no external modules, network access or media-processing dependencies. A video implementation must declare its own engine, dependency versions and rendering procedure. The sample package's runtime declaration covers only the helper.
